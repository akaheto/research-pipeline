# User Guide: Research Pipeline

Welcome to Research Pipeline! This guide will help you conduct better research faster.

---

## Quick Start (2 minutes)

### 1. Enter Your Research Topic
Type what you want to research in the search box. Examples:
- "best programming languages for machine learning"
- "competitive analysis of email marketing tools"
- "market trends in renewable energy"

### 2. Choose Your Research Method
**🔬 Perplexity + Claude**
- More thorough analysis
- Takes longer (~30-45 seconds)
- Costs about $0.02-0.05 per research
- Best for: Deep analysis, strategic decisions

**📰 Perplexity Only**
- Quick, direct answers
- Takes less time (~10-20 seconds)
- Costs about $0.01-0.02 per research
- Best for: Quick facts, market updates

**🎯 Frameworks**
- Pre-built research templates
- Asks 5 focused questions automatically
- Great for structured analysis
- Best for: Investment analysis, competitive intelligence

### 3. View Results
Your research appears as an organized report with:
- **Summary:** Key takeaways at the top
- **Findings:** Detailed information with sources
- **Cost:** Exact price of this research

## Advanced Features

### 📚 Research History
**View your past research**
- Click **📚** button (top right)
- See all past searches with dates
- Click any search to reload it
- Delete searches you don't need anymore

**Multi-Select for Synthesis:**
- Click checkbox next to searches
- Select 2 or more
- Click **✨ Synthesize** button
- Get unified analysis across searches

**Example:** 
- Search 1: "AI market growth"
- Search 2: "AI adoption by industry"
- Synthesize → Shows how adoption relates to growth

### 📚 Sources & Credibility
**Evaluate information quality**
- Click **📚 Sources** button in results
- See all sources ranked by credibility
- Color-coded badges:
  - 🟢 **Green (80%+):** Highly credible (academic, major news)
  - 🟡 **Orange (60-79%):** Credible (established companies)
  - 🔴 **Red (<60%):** Requires verification (blogs, forums)

**What affects credibility:**
- Website reputation (.edu, .org, news sites = higher)
- Domain type (.gov, academic = higher)
- Whether it's an official source

**Click any source** to see:
- Full credibility breakdown
- Where it appears in your research
- All citations from that source

### 🔍 Claim Verification
**Find unsupported statements**
- Click **🔍 Claims** button
- See all factual statements extracted
- Color-coded by confidence:
  - 🟢 **High:** Multiple sources, well-cited
  - 🟡 **Medium:** At least one source
  - 🔴 **Low:** Unsupported (no citations)

**Conflict Detection:**
- Look for ⚠️ badge showing conflicts
- Click to see contradicting claims
- Example: Source A says "50%", Source B says "35%"

### 📝 Notes
**Annotate your research**
- Click **📝 Notes** button
- Add thoughts, questions, or observations
- Notes are saved automatically
- Delete notes you no longer need

**Use cases:**
- Flag important insights
- Note questions for follow-up
- Track your own analysis
- Add context for team sharing

### 📈 Timeline
**Compare research over time**
- Click **📈 Timeline** button
- See recent research chronologically
- Click past research to compare
- See what changed since your last search

**Detects changes in:**
- Key statistics (numbers, percentages)
- Number of findings
- Main metrics

**Example:**
- January: "Market size $50B"
- March: "Market size $75B"
- Timeline shows the change

### 🎯 Custom Frameworks
**Create your own research templates**
- Click **🎯 Frameworks**
- Click **➕ Create Custom**
- Enter:
  - Name (e.g., "Startup Analysis")
  - Description
  - Pick an emoji icon
  - Add 1-10 research questions

**Your custom framework** will:
- Run all questions automatically
- Synthesize results together
- Save permanently for reuse

**Example Custom Framework:**
```
Name: Customer Research
Questions:
1. Who are the target customers?
2. What are their main pain points?
3. How do they currently solve this?
4. What would they pay for a solution?
5. What's the total addressable market?
```

## Common Questions

### Q: Why does one search cost more than another?
**A:** Cost depends on:
- How long the answer is (more tokens = higher cost)
- Which method you use (Perplexity+Claude costs more than Perplexity Only)
- The complexity of the question

Typical costs:
- Simple facts: $0.01-0.02
- Complex analysis: $0.03-0.10
- Frameworks: $0.05-0.20

### Q: Where do sources come from?
**A:** Perplexity searches the entire web and includes the sources it finds. Sources are real websites, news articles, research papers, and official sources.

### Q: How long do you save my research?
**A:** All research is saved for 30 days. After 30 days, it's automatically deleted. The only way to keep it longer is to download it (Word, PDF, or Markdown).

### Q: Can I share this with my team?
**A:** Yes! Download as Word or PDF and send. All sources are included. If you create custom frameworks, you'll need to tell them the framework details (they can create their own).

### Q: What if a claim seems wrong?
**A:** Click **📚 Sources** and check the credibility score. If the credibility is low, the source may not be reliable. Look for conflicts using **🔍 Claims**. Cross-reference with other sources.

### Q: Can I compare two different searches?
**A:** Yes! Use **📚 History**:
1. Open history
2. Click checkbox on two searches
3. Click **✨ Synthesize**

This creates a unified analysis showing how they relate.

### Q: How do I know if a source is reliable?
**A:** Check **📚 Sources**:
- Green badges (80%+) = Most reliable
- Orange badges (60-79%) = Credible but verify
- Red badges (<60%) = Requires verification

Credibility is based on domain type, reputation, and academic/official status.

### Q: Can I edit my notes?
**A:** Currently, you can only add and delete notes. To edit, delete the note and add a new one.

### Q: What if I run out of ideas for research?
**A:** Click **🎯 Frameworks** to see pre-built templates:
- **Investment Analysis** - Market, competition, risks, valuation
- **Competitive Analysis** - Features, pricing, positioning
- **Market Research** - TAM, growth, customer segments
- **Technology Stack** - Tech comparison and evaluation
- **Product Launch** - Strategy and planning

Each runs 5 structured searches automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### "Research failed"
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try a shorter, simpler question
- If it keeps failing, the API may be temporarily down

### "No sources found"
- The topic might be too niche
- Try a broader search
- Rephrase your question

### "Claim showing as unsupported"
- The model extracted a statement without nearby citations
- Check **📚 Sources** to see actual citations
- Some claims may just need independent verification

### "Can't find my research in History"
- History shows last 100 searches
- Very old research (30+ days) is deleted automatically
- Download important research as Word/PDF to keep
